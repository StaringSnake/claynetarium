## Decisions and changed paths

- `src/solar-view.js` blends pullback, travel and approach over the existing 900 ms. A new selection starts from the currently drawn target offset, radius, and orientation and follows the live destination. An interruption restarts easing without a pose snap; it does not carry velocity. `src/app.js` uses this for changed body selections, including the Sun returning to its wide default pose. The overview preset still acts immediately; reduced motion takes the same final pose without animation.
- The hover check shares exact hit testing with picking but distinguishes body hits from guide hits. Bodies take priority, including with guides off. A direct hit reuses the outline and underlined label; a guide hit also accents the line. A guide cannot hit when hidden. Click/tap still tests the actual release against the painted frame, independently of hover. Hover remains coalesced into one hit test per frame; an active hit is revalidated on a repaint.
- `tests/model.test.js` measures the rendered camera through the old boundaries and a mid-flight retarget. `tests/browser.test.js` exercises the normal desktop and emulated-phone paths, hover priority and clearing, exact input, and both initial and in-flight reduced-motion choices.

## Performance review follow-up

Reviewer → Frontend Implementer: the initial probe measured `renderer.render` alone and had only one cold-Earth trial per cell. The follow-up compared two scratch exports: the exact `a852aea9f5ed8b6f9b0fc69209cca2bc159626d8` base and that base with the original `review.patch` applied (SHA-256 `6516f4c8795cfb509238e4be4021267e8c03fc282e03da8544c485c974c7fe8d`). It did not use unrelated checkout edits.

- The warm probe records synchronous Solar draw from `updateSolar` through Three rendering and the cursor update. It separates scene-matrix plus raycast work, and counts raycasts and GPU draw submissions. A paused normal-speed Venus-to-Pluto move receives at most one synthetic mouse event during the transition; the pointer stays at the same screen coordinate thereafter. Cold Earth runs use a fresh browser/WebGL context with alternating variant/viewport order, but retain the same Chrome process and its shared caches.
- Reproduce from repo root with `PROBE_LOG_PREFIX=final node .git/info/solar-camera-polish/performance-review.mjs all 8` and `node .git/info/solar-camera-polish/performance-review-summary.mjs .git/info/solar-camera-polish/final-all-raw.json`. The run uses headless Chrome 158/SwiftShader on an Intel MacBookPro16,1 with desktop DPR 1 and iPhone emulation (native DPR 3, app render-buffer DPR capped at 2). The same synthetic mouse workload at both DPRs is not a claim of touchscreen hover. Timers measure synchronous main-thread work, not compositor presentation or hardware-GPU time.
- The initial repeat found a material regression beyond the raycast itself: the first guide hover on the reviewed build stalled mid-focus for 3.058 s on desktop and 3.878 s on phone emulation; respectively 3.055 s and 3.876 s were inside `renderer.render`. The remaining guide trials were already warm. Base ignored the same mid-move input; no-hover and warmed body/guide costs were small. The cold-Earth issue and first-hover issue are tracked separately.
- The full-app timing repeat reproduced this: first guide hover stalled for 4.649 s desktop / 3.379 s phone, of which all but 2 ms was inside `renderer.render`. No-input controls had 57–58 draws/move. The reviewed guide samples ran 2–4 checks/move; body samples ran 10/move with no guide pass; both were limited to one per frame. Matrix plus ray took at most 2 ms for guide and 3 ms for body, at 1 ms observed clock granularity. The first guide stalls cut the move to 40/39 submitted frames at the two sizes; pooled frame p95 of 1 ms would hide this.
- Cold Earth, 8 fresh contexts per build/viewport: median of each context's worst app draw was 915→945 ms desktop and 702→709.5 ms phone; observed maxima were 1001→965 ms and 734→722 ms. Nearest-rank p95 equals the maximum at n=8; it is not a stable tail estimate. A separate earlier batch went 964.5→878.5 ms desktop and 696→699.5 ms phone (n=8 per cell); the initial single-run desktop increase did not reproduce consistently. All 32 final-batch slowest frames started 7–16 ms after the Earth asset was ready, with zero hit tests. The largest instrumented WebGL call in those frames was `getProgramInfoLog` (median shader-call aggregate 504–596 ms, texture-call aggregate 2–2.5 ms by cell); prep was separately sliced. This localizes much of the stall to first-use shader/program work in SwiftShader, without claiming to account for every millisecond or isolate the driver's internals. No Earth source changed.
- `src/app.js` restores the base guard that ignores hover input during focus travel. The next mouse movement after settling still highlights a visible body or guide using the coalesced frame path; exact picks can still interrupt a move. This avoids introducing first-use highlight rendering into a camera transition instead of caching or eagerly compiling a shader. It does not remove the existing first-idle-hover cost. A regression in `tests/browser.test.js` fails against the reviewed source (`pointer` during the transition) and checks that body hover works again immediately after settling.
- `PROBE_LOG_PREFIX=first node .git/info/solar-camera-polish/performance-review.mjs hover-first 2` compared base, reviewed, and corrected bundles (two fresh contexts per build/viewport). The fixed build took 57 draws, zero hover checks, and at most 1 ms synchronous app draw in all four active moves; base took 57–58 draws, zero checks and at most 2 ms. The original review took 39–40 draws, two checks and peak app draws of 3.54–4.17 s desktop / 2.76–3.63 s phone. WebGL `getProgramInfoLog` was the largest instrumented shader call on each first highlighted frame. First intentional hover **after** settling still hit once and peaked at 3.15–3.84 s desktop / 3.06–4.27 s phone on base and 3.16–4.74 s / 2.78–3.67 s on the fix; this is a remaining SwiftShader first-use cost, not a speedup claim.
- Final checks on the corrected source: `npm test` 23/23, `npm run test:browser` built and passed 43/43, and `node --check` / `git diff --check` passed. Logs and raw measurements are in `.git/info/solar-camera-polish/`; see `performance-review-reproduction.md` there. No staging or commits.
- The original `review.patch` remains immutable. `performance-review-delta.patch` is relative to that reviewed tree; `review-with-performance.patch` includes the complete change relative to the base. The local experiment's `performance-code-only.patch` applies just the runtime guard.
