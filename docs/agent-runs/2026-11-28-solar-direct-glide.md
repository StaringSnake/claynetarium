## Camera decision

Keep the existing size-based landing frame and 900 ms duration. Interpolate the point of aim and short-axis framing radius together from the last painted pose to the live destination, with no intermediate wide frame. A smaller destination moves the camera closer, a larger one can need more room, and the Sun still restores the full overview and its default angle. Retargeting preserves the pose but starts a new easing curve rather than carrying velocity.

Keeping the same literal distance would make some bodies too small and could clip larger bodies or Saturn's rings. A straight glide is the default; a rotated camera lined up with a destination can bend sideways near the destination without changing the zoom curve. This is a local clearance guard, not general collision avoidance for other passing bodies, overlapping models, or abrupt simulation-time changes.

`src/app.js` and its input policies did not change. Hover does not run during travel; exact release-time picks, hover with paths hidden, reduced motion, playback, and observers retain their existing behavior.
